## Curve freshness and publication

The accompanying `verification-2026-11-03-downloads.json` records the UTC
check time, HTTP results, source hashes, maximum workbook dates and the
existing freshness guard's result. Both the archive and latest-month ZIP
were requested from the Bank with a no-cache header and parsed independently
in memory, without using the pipeline's local workbook cache. Each relevant
workbook's newest dated row and newest complete row were compared so a parser
silently discarding a newer incomplete observation would be visible.

The earlier 09:27 GMT direct check returned the 30 October curve from the
current-month workbook (HTTP 200; Last-Modified 2 November 11:04:35 GMT),
and SONIA 3.7352% dated 29 October. The guard passed: 30 October to
3 November is four calendar days and two business days, the allowed maximum.
The guard checks curve age, not whether a newer observation exists elsewhere,
and it does not apply a separate SONIA freshness test.

The Bank aims to publish each curve by noon the following business day.
Its public SONIA database updates by 10am on the business day after SONIA
is first published. At this morning's check, the source files themselves
still contained the old observations; this was not a local parser/cache
failure. This establishes the newest data served by the checked public
sources at the recorded time, not availability for the rest of lock day.
Re-fetch after noon and immediately before writing the actual lock. If
inputs change, regenerate m0 and update the proposed probability before
approval; do not carry 89.39% forward automatically.

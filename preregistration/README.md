# Analysis Plans

These documents distinguish proposed work from registered protocols and results.
A draft is not a pre-registration. Author approval, a dated DECISIONS entry and
a publicly pushed immutable tag must precede running a registered analysis.
Corrections after registration require a new version; the tagged original stays.

Both are intended for review before 3 November 2026. This is a deadline, not a
claim that either protocol is already registered. If a specification is not
ready, keep the existing v1 method; do not improvise a new one on lock day.

The historical results and two locked calls are not retrospectively relabelled
as confirmations of either new analysis. A risk-management dictionary designed
after reading the July and September episodes is exploratory on that history;
only untouched future outcomes can evaluate it prospectively.

The risk-management brief must specify tokenisation and matching, its unit of
analysis, conditional and negation rules, treatment of sentences scoring in
both dictionaries, collective versus member statements, score normalisation,
missing-data rules, model inputs and fitting, comparison benchmarks, loss,
multiple-testing policy, evaluation sample and a fixed stopping rule. No series
may be computed or used in a locked call under an unfinished brief.
